## Research Instruction

The research input requested an overview of world religions, including population distribution. Repository policy treats this as a durable research report, so the work was saved as synchronized Japanese and English article content with source notes and a research log.

## Process

1. Checked Pew Research Center's 2025 global religious composition estimates.
2. Cross-checked Pew's dataset page, country table, methodology page, and 2026 religious diversity report.
3. Used Our World in Data to confirm interpretation limits around self-reported affiliation and regional distribution.
4. Treated Pew's 2015 projection to 2050 as an older scenario because Pew revised its 2010 baseline in 2025.
5. Structured the article around global ranking, regional concentration, country-level diversity, projection limits, and measurement limits.

## Decisions

- The population table uses Pew's 2020 estimates.
- The article does not multiply 2020 religious shares by the July 2026 world population.
- The article uses "religiously unaffiliated" rather than "atheist" or "nonreligious."
- The regional section highlights Christian growth in sub-Saharan Africa, Muslim distribution across Asia and Africa, and Hindu concentration in India.
- The hero image is a derived bar chart of the population distribution rather than an abstract set of religious symbols.

## Follow-Up

- Update the projection section if Pew publishes new religious population projections based on the 2025 estimates.
- Denominational structure, religious practice frequency, and country-level religious law need separate reports.
