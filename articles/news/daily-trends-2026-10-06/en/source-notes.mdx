# source-notes

## Politics

- We selected five AP-led items spanning India, Kosovo, France, Nebraska, and Latvia because they all tie recent protests or voting pressure to institutional legitimacy.
- Older campaign commentary and lower-signal items were left out in favor of the clearest 24-48 hour stories.

## Economy

- The set covers U.S. jobs, U.S. equities, Schneider Electric’s PTC deal, euro weakness, and oil prices to capture the rate, currency, and capital-spending angle.
- We favored fresh market moves and new filings over older macro explainers.

## Technology

- The tech mix combines OpenAI, DeepSeek/Huawei, AI power constraints, an FBI breach involving an Accenture contractor, and legal-AI commercialization to show the shift from model hype to infrastructure and governance.
- We avoided thinly sourced rumors and used Reuters-linked or primary material where possible.

## Selection Notes

- Older summer political analysis and weaker-source topics were excluded.
- Every topic keeps at least one traceable source so the digest stays compact but source-backed.
