## Politics

- I downgraded the India voter-list protests and a few broader election stories because they overlapped with the same election-integrity theme and did not add as much fresh structure for this digest.

## Economy

- The economy set pairs softer inflation and weaker hiring with market strength and policy response. The set also includes industrial-policy messaging from Maryland and diesel-cost relief from Nebraska, because both were tightly tied to voter price pressure.
- I avoided overloading the section with near-duplicate market recaps and instead kept one stock-market read plus one broader cost-of-living policy move.

## Technology

- The tech set focuses on AI safety, labor protection, data-center backlash, and child-safety concerns. The common thread is that regulation is moving faster at the state and local level than at the federal level.
- The White House self-policing accord was considered, but the final mix favored stories with clearer public-policy stakes and stronger photo support.

## Selection Notes

- India’s election protest was considered but set aside in favor of Serbia because Serbia offered a cleaner election-admin angle and stronger image support.
- A Fed-timetable note was useful context, but the labor and inflation items already captured the macro signal, so it was not kept as a separate topic.
- I prioritized items with usable AP photo URLs and clear current relevance over broader background explainers.
