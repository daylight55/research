## Starting points and selection

The investigation separated developmental mechanisms, evolutionary retention, present capacities, cultural treatment, and judgments of value. It began with a human developmental review, then compared fetal observations, mouse tissue interactions, and mouse–guinea pig differences. Mathematical expressions were selected from relevant general theories rather than presented as nipple-specific laws.

## How evidence was combined

- **Shared development.** The human review and fetal observations support the core account. Sex determination and nipple formation are not reduced to a simple before-and-after sequence.
- **Species differences.** Male mouse loss and guinea pig retention provide a comparison. The 2025 paper was treated as unreviewed and does not establish a human mechanism.
- **Receptors and genetics.** Equilibrium binding requires free ligand, one site class, and equilibrium assumptions. The Lande-type expression describes short-term quantitative-trait responses; it does not estimate binary nipple presence or human parameters.
- **Physics.** Mouse mammary bud experiments and general surface mechanics serve different roles. The general equation cannot alone derive nipple number or human male retention.
- **Current capacities.** Student self-reports and one clinical case were included without extending them to population-wide physiology or ordinary lactation capacity.
- **Culture.** A 2010s ethnography and a Dani generational comparison address different questions. Norms, desire, and touch remain distinct; a nondetected difference is not proof of equivalence.
- **Philosophy.** Current causal roles are separated from selection history, and usefulness is not treated as a measure of personal value.

## Excluded explanations and limitations

The sources do not establish a single cause such as costly removal, evolution for pleasure, or retention through drift. The models clarify assumptions rather than fit measurements of human nipples. Abstract-only sources limit verification of methods and effect sizes. This is an explanatory investigation, not a systematic review or medical advice.

## Evidence inventory

### Javed & Lteif 2013

[Development of the Human Breast](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3706056/)

Review of human breast development. Supports shared early mammary development; it does not imply an absence of all sex differentiation until after nipples form.

### Kellokumpu-Lehtinen et al. 1987

[Ultrastructure of human fetal mammary gland](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3605662/)

Author abstract examined. Human fetal observations; conclusions are limited to the epithelium and mesenchyme studied.

### Heuberger et al. 1982

[Induction of androgen receptor formation by epithelium-mesenchyme interaction in embryonic mouse mammary gland](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC346327/)

Author abstract examined. Mouse tissue interactions and androgen receptor formation; not a direct human mechanism.

### Zheng et al. 2025

[Developmental basis of male nipple loss and retention in mammals](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12633513/)

Treated as an unreviewed preprint during this investigation. Mouse and guinea pig comparison; human extrapolation remains unestablished.

### Hulme & Trevethick 2010

[Ligand binding assays at equilibrium: validation and interpretation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3000649/)

Equilibrium binding theory and assay assumptions. Used for the single-site illustration, not to infer nipple fate from ligand concentration.

### Cheng & Houle 2020

[Predicting Multivariate Responses of Sexual Dimorphism to Direct and Indirect Selection](https://www.bio.fsu.edu/~dhoule/Publications/Cheng%20and%20Houle%2020.pdf)

Page 392, equation 2. Two-sex quantitative genetic response, reduced to one trait per sex. No human nipple parameter estimates.

### Kimura 1962

[On the Probability of Fixation of Mutant Genes in a Population](https://www.physics.rutgers.edu/~morozov/677_f2018/Physics_677_2018_files/Kimura_Genetics1962_classic.pdf)

Pages 715–716, equations 7–11. Fixation, selection, and drift. The weak-selection scale does not establish the historical cause of nipple retention.

### Trela et al. 2021

[Cell influx and contractile actomyosin force drive mammary bud growth and invagination](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8164091/)

Mouse mammary bud experiments involving cell influx and actomyosin contraction. Tissue mechanics evidence, not a theory of human nipple number.

### Salbreux & Jülicher 2017

[Mechanics of active surfaces](https://www.pks.mpg.de/fileadmin/user_upload/MPIPKS/group_pages/BiologicalPhysics/juelicher/publications/2017/MoAS2017.pdf)

PDF page 2, equation 7. General active-surface mechanics; the article neglects inertia. A complete tissue prediction also needs constitutive laws and boundary conditions.

### Levin & Meston 2006

[Nipple/Breast Stimulation and Sexual Arousal in Young Men and Women](https://labs.la.utexas.edu/mestonlab/files/2014/10/2006-Levin-Meston.pdf)

Questionnaire of 301 university students, including 148 males. The 51.7% result is sample-specific self-report, not universal physiological measurement.

### Yoshiyama et al. 1980

[A male patient with chronic renal failure and galactorrhea](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpnjnephrol1959/22/6/22_6_689/_article/-char/ja/)

Author abstract of one male patient with chronic renal failure and galactorrhea. A clinical case cannot establish ordinary infant-feeding capacity.

### Faust 2017

[Hair, Blood and the Nipple: Instagram Censorship and the Female Body](https://www.genderopen.de/bitstreams/2fbfbf5e-72fc-4753-aaaa-392775b0cd52/download)

Pages 159–170. Digital ethnography of Instagram and artists in the 2010s. Used historically, rather than as a statement of current rules.

### Stefanczyk et al. 2025

[Nudity Norms and Breast Arousal: A Cross-Generational Study in Papua](https://www.stir.ac.uk/research/hub/publication/2109733)

Author abstract examined. Cross-generational comparison of 80 Dani men. No randomized exposure assignment or equivalence reanalysis.

### Altman & Bland 2004

[Confidence intervals illuminate absence of evidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC404543/)

Methodological discussion of confidence intervals and absence of evidence. The equivalence margin in the article is explanatory; this source does not reanalyze the Dani study.

### Gould 1997

[The exaptive excellence of spandrels as a term and prototype](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC23474/)

Discussion of male nipples, shared development, by-products, and the distinction between origin and present usefulness. Used as an evolutionary interpretation, alongside developmental evidence.

### Cummins 1975

[Functional Analysis](https://mechanism.ucsd.edu/bill/teaching/w10/cummins.functions.1975.pdf)

Pages 759–761. Causal-role functional analysis; separates a contribution to present capacities from a claim about evolutionary origin.

### Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, revised 2026

[Teleological Notions in Biology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/teleology-biology/)

Specialist encyclopedia on biological teleology and selected-effect accounts. Philosophical background, rather than new embryological evidence.

## Foundations and interpretation of the conceptual figures

The four new figures are original SVG explanations of relationships and questions, rather than reproductions or tracings of source figures. Japanese and English use the same compositions, with their content also explained in prose and captions. These original diagrams use the repository's GPL-3.0-only license, separately from the licenses of research papers and experimental images.

- **Four questions.** An editorial map of development, evolution, current roles, and meaning. Shared development follows Javed & Lteif and Gould; the distinction between current roles and selection history follows Cummins and the specialist encyclopedia on biological teleology. Social norms and personal experience remain distinct subjects. The four domains are neither a strict disciplinary classification nor a causal sequence.
- **Shared developmental foundations.** Parallel male and female columns follow Javed & Lteif's review of human mammary development. Fetal primordia are distinct from mature nipples. The fetal observations were cross-checked to avoid a simple before-and-after story about sex determination and nipple formation. Body shape, cell counts, and developmental timing are not to scale.
- **Genetic correlation.** A general illustration of the two-sex response equation discussed by Cheng & Houle. The image states the assumptions of positive B, zero direct male selection, and positive female selection. Lines represent shared genetic effects, not direct transmission of a trait from a female individual to a male individual. The diagram neither predicts binary nipple presence nor estimates human parameters.
- **Cell mechanics.** Trela and colleagues' abstract and results on ring cells and actomyosin were rechecked. The schematic distinguishes cell influx from contraction of epidermal ring cells surrounding the mammary bud. Cell arrangements, cross-sections, and arrows explain mechanisms rather than reproduce microscopy or force measurements. This concerns mouse mammary-bud morphogenesis, not direct evidence explaining human male nipple retention.
