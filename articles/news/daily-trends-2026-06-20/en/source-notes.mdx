# source-notes

## Politics

- The main sources were Axios on the ceasefire, The Guardian on the U.S.-Iran talks, voting-rights litigation, the White House ballroom plan, and AP on the ocean observatories reversal.

## Economy

- I used the Fed statement, dollar strength, gasoline prices, UK borrowing, and mortgage rates to trace the same rate and geopolitical shock through markets and households.

## Technology

## Selection Notes

- I downgraded older rumor-only AI stories and generic market explainers in favor of pieces with a visible filing, funding move, or policy consequence.
